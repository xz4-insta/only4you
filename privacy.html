Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 16, 2026

At Only4You, we care about the privacy of our creators and surprise recipients. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and handle your information when you use our website.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you directly provide when creating a surprise page, including:

  • Sender and recipient names
  • Surprise text messages, photos, and voice notes
  • Quiz questions and answers
  • Transaction details for paid surprise options

2. How We Use Information

We use the collected information solely to build, host, and display your personalized surprise pages. Media uploads (photos/voices) are securely hosted and served to recipients. We do not sell or share your personal details with third-party advertisers.

3. Data Expiry & Deletion

Surprise pages expire automatically based on your selected plan (e.g., 24 hours or 3 days). Once expired, surprise pages are no longer accessible to the public.

4. Security

We use industry-standard secure services (Firebase, Cloudinary) to protect your surprises and verify payments securely via Razorpay.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@onlyy4you.netlify.app.
