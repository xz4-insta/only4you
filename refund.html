Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: July 16, 2026

At Only4You, we strive to deliver beautiful digital experiences. Please read our cancellation and refund policies carefully.

1. Digital Service Nature

Since Only4You offers immediate access to create and generate digital surprises, our services are generally non-refundable once the surprise has been successfully generated and hosted.

2. Refund Eligibility

Refunds are only issued in the following exceptional cases:

  • Double payments due to checkout errors or technical issues.
  • If a system failure on our part prevents the surprise from ever being generated or hosted.

3. Refund Process

To request a refund, please email us at support@onlyy4you.netlify.app within 24 hours of your purchase with your transaction details and payment ID. Verified refunds are processed within 5-7 business days to the original payment source.

4. Cancellation

Because generation of the surprise page is instant, cancellations of orders after payment verification are not supported.
